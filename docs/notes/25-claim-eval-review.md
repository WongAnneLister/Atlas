# 25 — Claim evaluation review fixes

**What**
I kept short fragments with nearby sentences and preserved common abbreviations.
I added the question to each claim and to the review sheet.
I capped copied sentences and reported counts for each kind of change.

**Why**
A short reply can lose its meaning when it is split from its explanation.
The question helps the reviewer understand what the answer refers to.
