# 25 — Model-drafted claim labels

**What**
I let a model draft labels for 230 real claims, with a short reason each. The author then checks a blind sample. The sample holds every drafted "unsupported" claim plus 40 random others.

**Why**
The author asked for help, because labelling every claim takes about an hour. A draft with a review keeps the labels honest. Each label records who made it.

**Number**
No number yet. I will measure it in commit 25b.

**Trade-off**
Unreviewed drafts are weaker than human labels. A model may share blind spots with the verifier it judges. So I report agreement with the author next to every verifier result.

**Likely follow-up question**
Q: Who labelled your claim set?
A: A model drafted the labels and the author reviewed a blind sample. I report how often they agreed. Drafts without review stay marked as drafts.
